Artist

Maya Chen

Painting · London · b. 1998

Maya paints large, quiet abstractions. Colour is laid in thin layers until a field of terracotta, bone or slate feels settled. Marks are few. She trained in textile design before moving to painting, and the canvases still have the patience of cloth. This is her first gallery representation.
