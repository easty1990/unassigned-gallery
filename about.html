About

The Unsigned Gallery shows work by artists who are not yet signed to large dealers. Some paint in a traditional way. Some do not. The room is kept simple so that either can sit in it.

We sell original paintings and works on paper. Prices are listed. To buy, send an enquiry — there is no basket and no account. The gallery handles shipping and, where useful, a viewing.

Artists are taken on slowly. If you would like to be considered, write with a small set of images and a few sentences about the work. We reply to everyone.
