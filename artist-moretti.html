Artist

Luca Moretti

Still life & paper · Bristol · b. 1996

Luca moves between traditional still life and torn-paper studies. The paintings are small and exact. The works on paper are looser: ink lines over washed indigo and ochre. He is drawn to surfaces, shadows, and the quality of light on simple objects.
