Artist

Amira Okonkwo

Figurative · Manchester · b. 1992

Amira works from life and from memory. Portraits are slow. Figures, when they appear in rooms, are often turned away. She uses oil, charcoal and occasional pastel, and is interested in the quiet moments between action.
